Q1-2026 Earnings Call

AI Summary
Earnings Call on May 15, 2026

Revenue: SMIC posted first-quarter revenue of $2,505 million, up 0.7% sequentially, with wafer revenue making up 93.9% of sales.

Margins: Gross margin improved to 20.1%, up 0.9 percentage points sequentially, helped by higher pricing and a better product mix.

Outlook: For the second quarter, management guided for revenue to rise 14% to 16% sequentially and gross margin to be 20% to 22%.

Demand: Management said demand was being supported by AI-related chips, power management, automotive-grade products, and customers building inventory amid supply concerns.

Capital plan: The company said it will not distribute profits for 2025, citing continued heavy investment needs in capacity expansion and R&D.

Quarterly performance

SMIC reported a stable first quarter, with revenue up 0.7% sequentially to $2,505 million and gross margin rising to 20.1%. Management said the quarter benefited from stronger pricing in some products and a better mix, while overall utilization remained high despite a small sequential decline.

Capacity and utilization

The company added close to 9,000 12-inch equivalent capacity during the quarter, but utilization fell 2.6 percentage points to 93.1%. Management said this was mainly because a new fab was still ramping and because some mobile phone demand shifted from the fourth quarter into the first quarter.

Demand trends

Management described solid demand for AI-supporting chips, power management, automotive-grade products, and specialty memory. It said capacity shortages in some areas and customer inventory building were helping orders, especially in China and in segments tied to AI, consumer electronics, and industrial applications.

Pricing and mix

Average wafer prices increased 2.5% sequentially, which management linked to steady pricing in stronger product categories and to customers accepting price increases in short-supplied products. This pricing improvement was a key driver of the margin increase and the better second-quarter margin outlook.

Product platforms

SMIC said its standalone flash and analog platforms are seeing strong demand, while automotive-grade BCD, ToF, Micro OLED, and ultra-low-power logic platforms are gaining traction. Management emphasized that these platforms support applications such as automotive LiDAR, handheld imaging, smart headsets, AI glasses, and cloud and edge AI.

Second-quarter outlook

Management guided second-quarter revenue to grow 14% to 16% sequentially, with gross margin expected at 20% to 22%. It said shipment volume and blended ASP should both improve meaningfully, and that the margin outlook is about 2 percentage points higher than the previous quarter's guidance.

Capital spending and dividends

The company said 2025 free cash flow after capex remained negative because it is still in a phase of expanding capacity and investing in R&D. On that basis, SMIC plans not to distribute profits for 2025, saying funds should continue to be directed toward core operations and long-term competitiveness.

Macro and supply chain

Management said the global macro environment remains uncertain and could affect supply chain costs, stability, and market expectations. It plans to respond with multi-channel procurement, stronger supply chain resilience, and flexible resource allocation to protect delivery quality.

The company's ToF products have been widely applied into automotive LiDAR and handheld imaging devices. The company's Micro OLED platform has been adopted in emerging products such as smart head-mounted displays and AI glasses. The company's ultra-low power logic process platform provides wired and wireless connectivity solutions for both cloud AI and edge AI applications.

Second, driven by overseas AI siphon effects, customers in consumer and IoT fields trying to secure capacity in Mainland China, with orders flowing back to domestic. Third, AI has also driven demand for chips in emerging fields such as ToF, electric vehicle, and robots, where local companies have actively explored the markets. Fourth, a peer for localization promoted the demand for domestic logic and network chips. The fifth reason is the price increase effects and customers' earlier inventory building on concerns over insufficient supply in the future, as mentioned before.

Under the backdrop of the market changes, the company's advantages of technological reserves, diversified platform layouts and flexible capacity conversion support its order taking capability. What is intrinsic value?

Stock intrinsic value is the real worth of a company's stock, based on its financial health and performance.

Instead of looking at the stock's current market price, which can change due to people's opinions and emotions, intrinsic value helps us understand if a stock is truly a good deal or not.

By focusing on the company's actual financial strength, like its earnings and debts, we can make better decisions about which stocks to buy and when.
